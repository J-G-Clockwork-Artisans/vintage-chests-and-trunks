VINTAGE CHESTS & TRUNKS

Timber Selection Edition

A Vintage Story content mod that makes chests and trunks visually match the type of wood they were crafted with, using dynamic texture blending to keep the mod compatible and lightweight.

Core Features

12 Vanilla Wood Variants Fully supports all vanilla wood types: Oak, Birch, Pine, Redwood, Ebony, Larch, Kapok, Maple, Purpleheart, Walnut, Bald Cypress, and Acacia.
44 Wildcraft Wood Variants Fully supports all unique wood types from Wildcraft: Trees and Shrubs and Wildcraft: Trees and Shrubs (Extended).
42 Biodiversity Wood Variants Fully integrates with the new wood types introduced by Biodiversity: Trees.
Wood-Typed Labeled Chests & Trunks NEW Write custom text labels on your chests and trunks using Charcoal or Chalk! Perfect for keeping your storage base beautifully organized and identifying resources at a glance.
Vanilla Coexistence Does not override vanilla containers; default chests and trunks remain craftable side-by-side with the new wood-typed versions.

Crafting Recipes

*Note: Placing nails in the center slot or mixing wood types results in a vanilla chest.

Wood-Typed Trunk

Typed Chest Typed Chest
Place two identical wood-typed chests side-by-side anywhere in the crafting grid (shapeless) to craft the corresponding wood-typed trunk.
*Note: Mixing different wood chests results in a vanilla trunk.

A Note on Mod Development

Some of the code in this mod was written with the help of AI tools, but this is far from a "vibe-coded" project. As a developer, I have invested hours of my own life orchestrating, analyzing, and directing this implementation. Every line of code, recipe modification, and asset patch was carefully designed, verified, and heavily tested in-game to meet the high standards of how this mod should look, feel, and behave.

This process has been an incredible learning experience for me. I am constantly diving deep into Vintage Story's modding patterns, and I am completely open to feedback! If you are a more experienced developer, please feel free to inspect the codebase directly on GitHub and suggest improvements or best practices.
